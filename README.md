An institutional-grade web curriculum and financial modeling suite covering Private Equity (LBO mechanics, quality of earnings, deal structuring) and Hedge Fund Operations (market-neutral architecture, factor exposures, prime brokerage, and regulatory compliance).

Built as a lightweight, zero-dependency single-page application (SPA) optimized for deployment via GitHub Pages, Netlify, or custom LMS embeds.

---

## 📌 Executive Overview

Unlike introductory personal finance or retail trading courses, **Buyside Mastery™** delivers Wall Street-standard financial engineering frameworks, audit-ready formulas, and institutional documentation:

* **Dynamic Financial Engines:** Real-time client-side calculation models for LBO returns attribution and multi-tier distribution waterfalls.
* **Master-Feeder & Fund Architecture:** Concrete legal structures across Delaware LPs, Cayman blockers, and Master Funds to mitigate UBTI/ECI.
* **Institutional Modeling Standards:** Wall Street formatting conventions (color-coded inputs, non-circular cash sweeps, factor-neutral pod risk controls).
* **Buyside Asset Vault:** Production of deal documentation, investment committee memos, and M&A diligence matrices.
